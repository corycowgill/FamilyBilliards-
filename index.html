FAMILY HOUSE RULES

Rack. Aim. Repeat.

  1. Find your angle. Point or tap the table. Arrow keys work too.
  2. Hold to power up. The gauge rises and falls. Release the shot button at the power you want.
  3. Keep your streak. Sink a ball to keep shooting. Miss or scratch and the turn passes.

Every object ball counts as one point, including the 8. Most points after all 15 balls are pocketed wins. On a scratch, pocketed balls count and the cue ball returns automatically.

Keyboard: hold and release Space or Enter while the table or shot button is focused. Esc cancels a charge.
